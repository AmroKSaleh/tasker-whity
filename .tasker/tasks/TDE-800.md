## OUTPUT — the agent's role and the autonomy boundary (settled 2026-07-28)

### Amendment to this task's stated "current answer"
"The agent is the engine (executes + validates)" is now half wrong. Q5 removed the judging half: **the agent runs checks and captures observed evidence, but it does not rule on what the evidence means where judgment is involved.** Engine, not judge.

### What the agent does (stated positively, not as the residue of Q7)
- **Carries the operation across its length** so the human doesn't have to hold it. This is the Q1 root and the only job nothing else can do.
- **Produces the artifacts.**
- **Runs checks and captures observed evidence** — the raw datum, never an assertion.
- **Maintains durable state** — writes outputs, notes and KB entries so the next step or session can start cold.

### The one line that does the most work
**The agent decides WITHIN the bar, never ABOUT the bar.** If it concludes a criterion is unreasonable, it halts and says so; it does not quietly relax it and proceed. Silently renegotiating the standard is the failure mode that would make the entire contract layer decorative.

### THE HALT RULE — stop on CONSEQUENCE, not on UNCERTAINTY
Two candidate triggers existed and the system leaned on both. **Uncertainty is abandoned as a trigger.** It depends on the agent's introspection, which is badly calibrated in exactly the wrong direction: it fails to stop when confidently wrong (the case that matters) and stops needlessly when merely unsure. **Consequence is knowable in advance**, is a property of the ACTION rather than of the agent's state of mind, and can therefore be enforced structurally instead of hoped for.

Aligns with Q5's blast-radius rule — both say spend caution where the damage is, not where the doubt is.

**The agent runs a flow to completion without asking, halting only at:**
- the **terminal output** (Q7 — unconditional)
- an **irreversible or expensive-to-undo action** (deploy, delete, send, publish, pay)
- a **failed check it cannot resolve within the retry budget**
- a **durable stop signal** (TDE-384)

Note the shape of the third: that is a halt on **observed failure**, not on **felt doubt**. Evidence, not introspection. Same principle.

### The residue: forks the bar doesn't cover
When the agent hits a decision the bar never specified, it does NOT gate — low-consequence forks aren't worth the human's attention, and high-consequence ones already trip the irreversibility trigger. But it must **LOG the fork** (append a note recording what was decided on the human's behalf and why). Decisions made in the human's absence stay visible; they just don't stop the run. Log the fork, don't gate on it.

### What this ratifies vs. changes
**Ratifies** what is already built and directive-encoded: prepare→confirm→execute, retry-cap→`ask_human`, honour `stop_flow`, and the unattended work-loop rule "never guess on destructive/irreversible actions" (already consequence-based) plus "don't ask — note the open question and continue" (already refuses uncertainty-gating).
**Changes:** the agent stops being the validator of judgment criteria (Q5), and any remaining "stop when unsure" instinct in the flow runner comes out.

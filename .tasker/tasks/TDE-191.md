DEFERRED design decision (parked 2026-06-05). Not a 137 blocker — 137 is dev/MCP-exclusive, so it ships fine as MCP-only.

## The question
Flow authoring, contract authoring/editing, and validation feedback currently live entirely in the MCP/conversational surface (the agent proposes, the human confirms in chat). Decide later whether any of it should also surface in the web-app UI:
- Viewing/editing a contract's rules visually (vs. only via the agent).
- The Blueprint view already visualizes the flow graph (read-only) — does it become an editing surface for contracts/edges?
- Where the human-confirm gate lives if there's a UI (in-app vs. in-chat).

## Why deferred
- 137 stays MCP-conversational; a UI is additive, not required for the foundation.
- The Blueprint view (read-only flow graph) is the natural seed if/when we do this.

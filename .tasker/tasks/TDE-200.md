WEAKNESS (from the 2026-06-05 flow review): the executing agent authored the contracts AND graded them, so every gate passed — including a blocker rule it objectively failed (TDE-196's "documents every tool with its parameters"). Self-grading has no teeth.

FIX: route validation through an INDEPENDENT perspective — a fresh agent/subagent with no stake in passing — and prefer EXECUTION-BASED checks (run the artifact, observe) over assertion-based ones (mirror Claude Code's `verify` skill). This is the single most important fix for the contract layer to mean anything. Pairs with TDE-156 (revision loop) and #adversarial-judge below.

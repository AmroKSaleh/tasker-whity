Currently the Projects homepage opens to a random project. Desired behavior: on first open of the app (per app session), show the Orgs view instead. On every subsequent click into the Projects homepage during that same session, open the Environment that the user's current active project belongs to. This "first open shows Orgs" state resets whenever the app is closed (i.e. it's session-scoped, not persisted across app restarts).

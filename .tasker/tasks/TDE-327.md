Deferred half of TDE-324. TDE-324 shipped TASK relay (a curated `relay_context` hand-off layer on a single task, MCP-first). The original task title was "relay a task/project" — project-level relay was deliberately scoped out because it's a much bigger, underspecified surface.

Why needed: same intent as task relay (hand off work to someone without a follow-up round-trip), but for a whole project — its Foundation, sections, tasks, flows, KB. We already have full-fidelity project export/import (export_project / import_project bundle; KB db99e314). The open question is whether "project relay" is just "export bundle + a project-level relay note," or something richer (per-task relay layers, a guided hand-off, recipient routing).

Resolve into a concrete task/spec once the questions below are settled with the user.

--- ZONE 3 DECISION REVERSED (2026-07-01, via Gemini triangulation) ---
Vertical-stacked queue is OUT. Keep the HORIZONTAL KANBAN columns. Reasons: (1) triage is a comparative act — side-by-side columns beat a long vertical scroll for weighing options; (2) my only real justification for vertical was "horizontal hides sections 7-11", but that's better solved another way (below) without losing column comparison + within-column drag.

NEW Zone 3 = horizontal kanban + a persistent compact "SECTION OVERVIEW / INDEX" strip that shows ALL sections' counts/pressure at once AND teleports to off-screen columns. Rationale: neither raw layout shows all 11 sections at once (kanban shows ~6, vertical shows ~1-2), so the overview strip is the only thing that gives true all-sections density — it's the god's-eye view; the columns stay the working/drag surface. This beats both pure-vertical and a plain teleport-only minimap.

Unchanged: Zone 1 Pulse (loud metric), Zone 2 Now Band (promote in-progress/needs-you out of rail), Zone 4 Controls (3 clusters), dissolve right rail, single-object board (Gemini concurred empty space = clarity).

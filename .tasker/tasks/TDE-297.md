Tasker should have guardrails for concurrent creation — when two agents/sessions create tasks or flows at the same time, short-IDs collide. Likely to happen in team projects. Solution approach TBD, but it needs handling at the Tasker level (e.g. atomic/serialized short-ID assignment, optimistic-lock + retry, or reserving IDs on create).

Source: feedback from a friend who hit it live. Their report:

"Renumbered WC-233 → WC-237. A concurrent session merged SDUI SP3 as WC-233–236 while I was working (WC-233 looked free at my start). I rebased onto current main (only the 2 generated files conflicted — regenerated core-only), squashed, re-verified, and closed the stale PR #309."

The collision happened because two agents were creating two flows at the same time — WC-233 looked free when the second session started, but the first had already taken WC-233–236, forcing a manual renumber to WC-237. Easily reproducible in team/multi-agent projects.

Design analysis from 2026-07-28, captured so it is not lost. NOT ready to start — see the blocker at the bottom.

THE FRAMING: the Flows page today is a cockpit for INSPECTING CONTRACTS. The definition settled on TDE-792 wants a cockpit for CARRYING A LONG OPERATION. Same data, different page. TDE-813 made the page stop lying (ungated flows are visible, badges no longer claim guarantees nothing provides); that is triage, not design.

WHAT IS ACTUALLY OFF

The graph is the centrepiece and the new definition demotes it. FlowGraph occupies the top 320px by default and renders the I/O DAG. Edges are now optional and rare, so a gateless flow renders as a scatter of disconnected nodes eating a third of the screen while saying nothing. The graph assumes the DAG IS the flow — it is not; the flow is an ordered operation and edges are merely where gates sit. The step list should be primary and the graph conditional on there being edges worth drawing.

The gate badge is the headline trust signal, and contracts are no longer the point. A flow card shows steps, done count, blessing state. The questions that matter now are "how far through this long operation am I" and "where does it need me". Blessing is a minor property of a few seams.

The biggest gap is what is not there at all: EXCEPTIONS. Q7 (TDE-799) settled that the human authors the bar once, then reviews exceptions rather than outputs. The page does the exact opposite — every contract inline, everything expanded, nothing about what needs you. A flow detail should open with "step 4 failed its check, here is the evidence" and leave the rest collapsed. This is TDE-382, promoted from presentation to structural for exactly this reason.

Smaller: progress reads "2/5 DONE" as though the 5 is knowable (TDE-811), and the terminal step gets no special treatment despite Q7 making it the one unconditional gate.

BLOCKED ON: the rebuild-or-refine decision, still open on TDE-792. Redesigning this page before deciding whether flows get rebuilt is a good way to do it twice. The one piece worth doing regardless of how that lands is exception surfacing (TDE-382), because every version of the new model needs it.

## DECISION 2026-08-04 — REFINE, not rebuild. UNBLOCKED. Scope fixed below.

**The stated blocker was stale.** This task said "BLOCKED ON: the rebuild-or-refine decision, still open on TDE-792." TDE-792 is DONE — closed out 2026-07-30, two days after this analysis was written. Its close-out record covers the canonical definition, the TDE-808 directive shipped to main, and a keep/cut/reshape re-judging of the parked Flows tasks. It never recorded a rebuild-or-refine decision, because by then the question had already been answered by action rather than by decree.

**Why refine is the answer, from the record and the code:**
1. Three refine actions have already shipped against the new definition. TDE-808 shipped a new directive rather than a new model. TDE-813 reworked the trust badges. TDE-820 changed gate semantics so contracts no longer block finalization. None of the three required or implied a rebuild — the data model absorbed the redefinition without complaint.
2. `GateBadge` in FlowsPage.jsx (lines 18–64) is careful, current, new-definition-aligned code: four honest states, `edgeCount === 0` treated as legitimately Ungated rather than a failure, `gateBypassed` re-worded as provenance-not-violation with the TDE-820 reasoning in the comments. You do not throw away work that was done correctly six days ago.
3. Decisive structural point: **the parts that must change are not the parts that carry the debt.** FlowsPage.jsx is 660 lines and `FlowDetail` alone is ~430 of them, but that bulk is five inline CRUD mutations — duplicate, delete, rename, short-id assign, save-as-template. The redefinition touches none of it. Every change this task wants lands somewhere small and mostly additive.

**Part of the original analysis is now stale too — re-check before working.** It says "FlowGraph occupies the top 320px by default" with the step list buried. That is no longer the layout. FlowsPage.jsx lines 503–556 are already a two-pane split: graph on the left (`flex-1`), and a resizable ~420px right pane explicitly commented "Action Center / Cockpit" holding `LiveStatusBanner` + `FlowStepList`. Someone already built a cockpit pane. The step list is co-primary today. The surviving critique is weighting — the graph still takes the larger, non-collapsible pane — not burial.

**What is genuinely still missing, verified in code:**
- **Exceptions: absent.** `LiveStatusBanner` (lines 66–111) reports COMPLETE / IN PROGRESS / WAITING and mentions contracts needing blessing. That is status, not exceptions. Nothing anywhere surfaces "step 4 failed its check, here is the observed value." This is the real work and it is additive — a new component in the right pane above `FlowStepList`. The MCP side already exists (get_flow_exceptions, TDE-382), so the data shape is settled.
- **Progress denominator lies.** `{doneCount}/{stepCount} DONE` appears twice — FlowCard line 133 and FlowDetail line 374 — with no regard for step_list_open (TDE-811). Two-line fix once the flag is read through.
- **Terminal step gets no special treatment**, despite Q7 making it the one unconditional gate. Lives inside FlowStepList.jsx (190 lines, isolated).
- **Graph should be conditional.** Gate it on `flow.gate.edgeCount > 0`, which is already computed and already used by GateBadge — a conditional at lines 534–536, not a rewrite.

**Explicitly OUT of scope (do not bundle):** splitting `FlowDetail`'s 430 lines and extracting its five CRUD mutations. It violates the project IS's ~150-line guideline and should be filed as its own debt task, but it is untouched by the redefinition and this task is on the launch critical path. Refactoring adjacent code while unblocking a launch blocker is how a small change becomes a large one.

**Status: unblocked, scoped, promoted to `high` and moved from Backlog into the Flows section** (its type home — per TDE-790's own principle that launch-blocker status is a label, not a section).

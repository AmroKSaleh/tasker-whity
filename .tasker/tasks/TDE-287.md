The moat work for Flows: make contract quality structural instead of dependent on agent diligence. Origin: 2026-06-19 strategy discussion + the 2026-06-15 flow-authoring dogfooding report.

DIAGNOSIS (what the evidence actually showed):
- Discovery protocol EXISTS but is unenforced — build_new_flow dispenses a grill-me interview playbook the agent can (and did) silently skip (report F1/F8).
- Distillation tooling EXISTS and is the best part of the UX — the set_task_output/set_task_input rule-quality linter flags vague judgment language at authoring time (report P1). Not the gap.
- I/O governance was NEVER wired — output def-of-done (set_task_output) and consumer acceptance criteria (set_task_input) are authored as two independent rule sets; the consumer's input requirement does not govern/seed the producer's output contract (report P2). This was the original design intent of the I/O system, unrealized.
- Human verification of the contract itself is unused — confirm_contract was called 0 times; all 12 contracts stayed AI-QA'd (report N1). An AI authors the bar and an AI judges against it: a closed loop with no human, the structural form of the false-pass risk.

KEY INSIGHT: discovery + distillation + derive-from-I/O + assumption-surfacing are ONE mechanism, not four. The authoring step becomes a single loop: AI reads the consumer's input requirement -> derives a draft output contract from it -> linter sharpens vague rules -> AI surfaces its assumptions/uncertainties -> human confirms/edits -> blessed.

RELATION TO TDE-203 (done): TDE-203 promised "a real, non-bypassable gate" for human-confirm but only shipped the provisional STAMP + warning, not the GATE. This task finishes that promise (warning -> enforced gate) and adds the derive-from-input + assumption-surfacing loop around it.

GUARDRAIL: enforced by default, bypassable ONLY via explicit human command, and a bypass must be recorded in the ledger so a weak/bypassed flow is visibly weak (not silently). Do not turn this into a heavy questionnaire — friction is what made the interview get skipped.

DEFERRED: richer/longer discovery investment waits until we measure whether this loop actually moves the false-pass / false-fail (revision-loop spin) numbers.

On /dashboard/:slug at 390px, the board header is unusable:
- The project title "Tasker Development" renders as "T… D…" — each word truncated to a single character on its own line.
- The header action row (CREATE TASK, ⌘K, SUMMARY, KB, IS, FILES, QUEUE, LOCAL) overflows the right edge. "SUMMARY" is cut mid-word and everything after it is entirely off-screen with no way to scroll to it.
- The navigation rail eats ~110px of the 390px viewport.

Note: the document itself does not scroll horizontally (scrollWidth === clientWidth), so the overflow is inside a container — a naive document-level overflow check will NOT catch this. That is why the automated sweep reported overflowX=0 while the page is visibly broken.

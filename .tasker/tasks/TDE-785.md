Mined from Plane's Cycles (their sprint primitive).

DO NOT steal Cycles as sprints — burndown, velocity, scrum ceremony is human-PM in the conceded coordination lane. SKIP that entirely.

THE SALVAGEABLE PRIMITIVE: the underlying container — "a bounded window of work with a start/end and a durable progress readout." Recast for us as a way to scope a BATCH of agent-executed tasks over a session / day / week, producing a durable progress artifact at the end. Fits "durable state across sessions" and pairs naturally with the day/session summary work (TDE-160/TDE-584) and the agent-draft→human-publish pattern.

WHY LOW / STUDY-ish: real risk of accidentally rebuilding sprints. Only worth it if the "work window" gives agents/humans something the existing section + summary machinery doesn't. NEXT STEP: decide whether a time-boxed work-window object earns its place OR whether sections + Day Summary scopes (TDE-584) already cover it — likely the latter; close as "already have it" if so. Cross-ref TDE-584.

Settled during the TDE-804 phases discussion. A cycle is bounded by the CALENDAR and repeats uniformly; a phase is bounded by an ACHIEVEMENT and does not. They answer different questions, so cycles were never a substitute for phases — but the temporal-axis need that motivated this task is now served by phases (TDE-804), and the durable end-of-window artifact is served by Day Summary scopes (TDE-584). What remains unique to a cycle is the fixed-length time-box, which is precisely the sprint/burndown/velocity ceremony this task said to skip — human PM coordination, the lane deliberately conceded to Linear/Jira.

Nothing to build. Reopen only if a real user asks for a fixed-length window that phases cannot express.

From Linear: Triage is a PER-TEAM intake inbox (not app-wide — same shape as our per-connector decision) with four keyboard verbs: Accept (to team default status), Mark as duplicate (merge into existing, transfer attachments), Decline (cancel with optional comment), Snooze (hide until a chosen time OR until new activity arrives, whichever comes first — the activity-wake wrinkle is the clever part).

Build into Conductor (stays per-connector; the killed app-wide Inbox stays killed): formalize the verb set on intake items — Accept→Placed, Decline→closed with a stored reason, Snooze→Parked with a wake time OR wake-on-new-activity, and duplicate-merge at intake (consumes merge_task_as_duplicate from the Duplicate defense task). Keyboard-driven in the Conductor panel.

Today Conductor has the three lifecycle tabs but no decline-with-reason, no timed/activity wake for Parked, no dedup at the gate. Intake hygiene = the front door of durable state.

WEAKNESS: in the test run, "don't ask for confirmation" silently skipped the human authoring/confirming the contracts — so the agent authored its OWN bar, removing the "external, human-authored" property that is the entire differentiation of Flows.

FIX: a contract is only "active/blessed" once a human has confirmed (or authored) it. Agent-only-authored contracts are marked PROVISIONAL and cannot gate as if human-approved. Surface contracts for human review/edit (ties to #flows-page-interactivity and #team-contracts-author-not-executor). Bias-to-action is fine for execution, but the contract bar itself must stay human-owned.

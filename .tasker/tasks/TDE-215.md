The independent validator subagent produces a critique (per-rule notes with evidence) when grading a handoff. Right now this lives only in the validation ledger buried in raw JSON. The user should be able to see it cleanly — both the per-rule verdict and the validator's reasoning — without digging into raw output data. This could surface in the Flows page step list (expanded view), in get_validation_feedback output, or as a dedicated UI panel. The critique is the most valuable output of the two-agent system: it tells the executor exactly why the work failed and what to fix.

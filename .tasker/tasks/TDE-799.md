## OUTPUT — the human's role (settled 2026-07-28)

**The human spends attention exactly three times, and nowhere else.**

### 1. Once, up front — AUTHORING THE BAR
The bulk of the human's judgment goes here, at flow-build time, with the AI. The interview's job is to interrogate every criterion with **"how would we check this?"** and record it as a judgment rule ONLY when no check exists.

Why this is the leverage point: a contract states **what must be true**; it cannot state **whether it is true of this output**. So authoring does not remove the act of looking — but every criterion converted into a check is one the human never looks at again, at every seam, on every run, forever. **The contract's job is to shrink the residue.** What cannot be reduced to a check IS the human's review list.

This also re-reads Round 1 (TDE-212) correctly: "contract proven, validation not" = the contract was doing the work, the validator was ceremony stacked on top.

### 2. During the run — REVIEWING EXCEPTIONS, NEVER OUTPUTS
The human is shown failed checks with their evidence, plus the unverifiable residue — not "here is step 3's output, approve it" but "step 3 failed rule 2, here is what was observed."

This is the only shape in which "a gate at every seam" and "a human who is still paying attention" can both be true, because the review load then scales with **problems found** rather than with flow length.

### 3. At the end — GATING THE TERMINAL OUTPUT, UNCONDITIONALLY
No receiver exists inside the flow to catch it, so this gate is never skipped regardless of what the checks said.

### What the human is explicitly NOT
- **Not a per-step approver.** Attention degrades with volume (29 reviews → rubber-stamping by the sixth), which manufactures blessed errors — the exact outcome gating exists to prevent. It also reintroduces the constraint flows exist to relieve (Q1: operations too big for one context); a 30-step flow with 29 human gates is the human doing the work with extra bookkeeping.
- **Not an observer.** Watching progress is what the flow view is for; watching is not a role in the gate.

### Human-as-EXECUTOR
Orthogonal to the three attention acts above, and already settled: `executor` is a per-step attribute (Q6), so a human executing steps is not a distinct role in the gate design and not a flow type. Guide Mode is the all-`user` case.

### Placement priority where the human does look mid-flow
**Blast radius** — early seams in long flows, and irreversible actions (see the Q5 correction, TDE-797).

FIXED & verified live (2026-06-09).

Root cause: a task whose group_id points to a group NOT in its own section (stale/foreign group_id) was excluded from the ungrouped bucket (it has a group_id) AND from every group in its section (its group isn't there) → silently dropped from the board. The section header totalCount still counted it (the "9 shown as 7" gap). NOT the filed name-dedup hypothesis — render is id-based and correct; the data was inconsistent.

Fix: ProjectBoard.jsx enrichedSections — ungroupedTasks now includes tasks where group_id is null OR group_id is not among the section's own group ids. Built + deployed to Netlify. User confirmed WCC-27 now shows.

Upstream cause of the bad data: TDE-180 (delete_group with delete_tasks:false doesn't clear group_id on member tasks). Render fix is the durable defense; TDE-180 should be fixed to stop creating orphaned group_ids.

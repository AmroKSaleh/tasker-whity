DECIDED 2026-06-19 (follow-up to TDE-287). Run-time validation stays SOFT (warn + provisional ledger stamp + cockpit badges); NO hard block on run_flow — too much friction for legacy/bypassed flows. The one hole to close is the sneaky drift case:

PROBLEM: editing a contract (set_task_input/output, or the app's saveContracts) resets confirmed:false, but the flow stays named and may have already been run/validated. So a previously-blessed, possibly-already-passed flow can silently degrade to AI-QA'd without anyone noticing.

MITIGATION ALREADY IN PLACE: the cockpit GateBadge recomputes live, so an edit flips the flow back to "N to bless". That's passive.

MAKE IT LOUD:
- App: when editing a contract that WAS blessed, warn inline that saving will un-bless it and require re-blessing (don't let the reset be silent).
- MCP/get_task: flag when a named flow's contract was edited AFTER its last bless or validation — a "contract changed since last blessed/validated" drift indicator — so a re-run knows the bar moved and the prior verdict is stale.

NOT doing: run-time hard block / escalate-to-ask_human on unblessed gates (considered, rejected as too heavy). Reference: the run-time provisional path in mcp/index.ts (isProvisional / provisional_warning) and [[Flows: contract-gate logic is mirrored in TWO places]].

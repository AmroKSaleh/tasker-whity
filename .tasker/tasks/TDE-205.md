WEAKNESS: rules like "non-technical readable" are too soft to enforce and trivially self-pass.

FIX: authoring guidance + light structure that pushes toward sharp, checkable rules — prefer `check` with concrete params; for `judgment`, require an objective-ish criterion + a stated way to verify it. Consider a "vague rule" lint at authoring time. The grill-me interview (TDE-189) should actively coach the user toward testable rules rather than accepting fuzzy ones.

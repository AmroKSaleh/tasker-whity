Mined from Azure DevOps Boards' work-item history (every field change recorded with who + when, first-class and queryable).

WHAT: A comprehensive, queryable audit of state changes across the spine — especially who changed which CONTRACT / gate / review state and when.

WHY IT FITS OUR WEDGE: our whole pitch is durable state across sessions, agents, and people. A complete audit of who-changed-what-when is directly on-brand — it's what makes "shared across people/agents" trustworthy. We already have adjacent pieces (flow audit via get_flow_audit, session activity via append_session_activity), so this is consolidation/promotion to first-class, not net-new philosophy.

OPEN QUESTION: is this an extension of the existing flow-audit + session-activity machinery, or a new unified audit layer over all task field changes? Resolve before scoping. Pointer: get_flow_audit, append_session_activity, get_task_activity already exist — map coverage gaps against them first.

RESCOPED 2026-06-19: this is NOT a full Gmail inbox client (that was the destination trap). Two parts:
1. Connect Gmail via the Connectors framework (gmail.readonly first). Depends on TDE-245 (Google OAuth foundation, code-complete).
2. A SCOPED sidebar panel surfacing only the Tasker-relevant slice of Gmail — e.g. recent/starred/unread threads you can CONVERT INTO TASKS, and emails linked to existing tasks.

Tasker Is Here: Task Management That Actually Speaks AI

If you've spent the last year pair-programming with an AI coding assistant, you already know the secret nobody warns you about: the bottleneck moved. Writing code got faster. Keeping track of what to build next did not.

You know the dance. You open Claude Code or Cursor, describe what you want, watch the magic happen. Then you switch to your task app to mark something done, copy-paste a description into a ticket, paste it back into your AI, re-explain the context your assistant already had thirty seconds ago. Tab, copy, paste, re-explain. Repeat. Your AI moves at the speed of thought, and your task tracker moves at the speed of clipboard.

Today, that ends. Tasker is now available.

A task manager built for how you actually work

Tasker is a task manager designed from the ground up for people who build alongside AI coding assistants. Instead of living in a separate tab that your AI can't see, Tasker connects directly to the tools you already use, like Claude Code and Cursor, so your tasks and your AI workflow stay in sync automatically.

No more copy-pasting. No more re-explaining context your assistant already worked through. Your tasks become something your AI can read, update, and act on, right inside the flow where you're already working.

What that looks like in practice

Picture this. You ask your AI assistant to tackle a feature. As it works, Tasker keeps up: tasks move from pending to in-progress to done, milestones check themselves off, and the context around each task, what it needs as input and what it produces as output, travels with it. When you come back tomorrow, you're not reconstructing where you left off. Your task board already knows.

Because Tasker connects through the same protocol your assistant already speaks, you can do it all in plain language. "Break this section into tasks." "What's blocking the launch work?" "Mark the auth flow done and pick up the next thing." Your AI reads from and writes to Tasker as naturally as it reads your code. The task app stops being a place you visit and becomes part of the workspace you live in.

And when you want the bird's-eye view, it's right there: a clean web app where you can see your projects, sections, and tasks laid out, reorder priorities, and watch the whole thing update as you and your assistant work together.

Why we built this

We built Tasker because we were living the problem. AI assistants are astonishingly good at doing the work, but they've been flying half-blind about which work matters and where it stands. Every existing task tool assumed a human would be the one clicking the buttons. None of them assumed your most productive collaborator might be an AI that never opens a browser.

So we flipped the assumption. Tasker treats your AI assistant as a first-class user, not an afterthought. The result is a task manager that finally keeps pace with the way modern building actually feels: fast, conversational, and continuous.
